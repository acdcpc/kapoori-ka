## Full Description — English (max 4000 characters)

कपूरी क (Kapoori Ka) is a bilingual child health tracking app built for Nepali families. It helps parents monitor their child's growth, vaccinations, and development from birth through age 5 — all available in both English and Nepali, with the Bikram Sambat calendar.

**Features:**

📈 **WHO Growth Charts** — Track your child's weight and height against WHO Child Growth Standards. See percentile curves for boys and girls. Enter measurements manually and watch your child's growth over time.

💉 **Nepal Immunization Schedule** — Complete Nepal National Immunization Program (NIP) vaccine schedule built in. Track which vaccines your child has received and which are coming up. Dates support both AD and Bikram Sambat calendar. Vaccine reminders help you never miss a dose.

🧠 **Developmental Milestones** — Track your child's development across motor, language, cognitive, and social domains. Based on WHO and CDC developmental milestone standards with over 100 milestones from birth to 5 years.

🧩 **M-CHAT Autism Screening** — Modified Checklist for Autism in Toddlers, Revised (M-CHAT-R/F). A validated 20-question screening tool for children aged 16–30 months, with Nepali translation.

📄 **PDF Reports** — Generate and share growth reports as PDF documents.

🌐 **Bilingual (नेपाली / English)** — Full English and Nepali language support. All vaccine dates, growth charts, and milestones are available in both languages.

**Security & Privacy:**
- All data encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
- Row-Level Security (RLS) on all database tables — your data is only accessible to you
- Email confirmation required for all accounts
- Session tokens stored in encrypted device storage

**Note:** This app provides reference information based on WHO and Nepal Ministry of Health standards. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for health decisions about your child.

💉 **नेपाल खोप तालिका** — पूर्ण नेपाल राष्ट्रिय खोप कार्यक्रम (NIP) तालिका समावेश। तपाईंको बच्चाले कुन खोप पाइसकेको छ र कुन आउँदैछ भनेर ट्रयाक गर्नुहोस्। मितिहरू AD र विक्रम संवत् दुवै क्यालेन्डरमा समर्थित छन्। खोप रिमाइन्डरहरूले तपाईंलाई कुनै पनि खोप नछुटाउन मद्दत गर्छ।

🧠 **विकासात्मक माइलस्टोनहरू** — मोटर, भाषा, संज्ञानात्मक र सामाजिक क्षेत्रहरूमा तपाईंको बच्चाको विकास ट्रयाक गर्नुहोस्। जन्मदेखि ५ वर्षसम्म १०० भन्दा बढी माइलस्टोनहरू सहित WHO र CDC मापदण्डहरूमा आधारित।

🧩 **M-CHAT अटिजम स्क्रिनिङ** — Modified Checklist for Autism in Toddlers, Revised (M-CHAT-R/F)। १६-३० महिनाका बच्चाहरूको लागि प्रमाणित २०-प्रश्न स्क्रिनिङ उपकरण, नेपाली अनुवाद सहित।

📄 **PDF रिपोर्टहरू** — PDF कागजातको रूपमा वृद्धि रिपोर्टहरू उत्पन्न र साझा गर्नुहोस्।

🌐 **द्विभाषी (नेपाली / English)** — पूर्ण अंग्रेजी र नेपाली भाषा समर्थन। सबै खोप मितिहरू, वृद्धि चार्टहरू र माइलस्टोनहरू दुवै भाषामा उपलब्ध छन्।

**सुरक्षा र गोपनीयता:**
- सबै डेटा ट्रान्जिटमा (HTTPS) र आराममा इन्क्रिप्टेड

**नोट:** यो एपले WHO र नेपाल स्वास्थ्य मन्त्रालयको मापदण्डहरूमा आधारित सन्दर्भ जानकारी प्रदान गर्दछ। यो व्यावसायिक चिकित्सा सल्लाहको विकल्प होइन। तपाईंको बच्चाको स्वास्थ्य निर्णयहरूको लागि सधैं योग्य स्वास्थ्य सेवा प्रदायकसँग परामर्श गर्नुहोस्।
